Services & Rates

I offer psychotherapy for adolescents (13–18 years old), adults, and couples in a warm, collaborative, and non-judgmental environment.

Individual Therapy

In-person • Online • 50 minutes

Tailored psychodynamic and skills-based psychotherapy for adolescents and adults dealing with emotional difficulties, life transitions, or mental health concerns. A safe space to explore your inner world at your own pace.

Couples Therapy

In-person • 50 or 90 minutes

A dedicated space to explore relational tension, rebuild communication pathways, and work through conflicts collaboratively. I help couples develop deeper understanding and connection.

Session Fees & Medical Aid

Session fees are in line with standard medical aid rates for psychotherapy. I am registered with all major medical aids and can assist with claims for Prescribed Minimum Benefits (PMBs) where applicable.

Please get in touch to discuss any questions you may have around rates, medical aid coverage, or affordability options.
