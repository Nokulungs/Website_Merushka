Clinical Practice & Background

Welcome. I am a registered Clinical Psychologist providing compassionate, evidence-based psychotherapeutic care. My goal is to foster a collaborative environment where you feel truly heard and supported through life's complexities.

Every individual's journey is unique. My practice centers on understanding your personal narrative, identifying patterns that may be causing distress, and helping you build practical coping strategies that promote long-term resilience and mental well-being.
